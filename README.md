# ♻ EcoBin Rewards

## Recycle Smart. Earn Rewards. Build a Sustainable Future.

EcoBin Rewards is an AI-powered recycling platform designed to encourage proper waste segregation and increase recycling participation. The platform combines AI-assisted waste verification, recycling activity tracking, EcoPoints, rewards, and leaderboards to make sustainable behavior more engaging and measurable.

---

## 🌍 Problem

Improper waste disposal and poor recycling participation are major environmental challenges in colleges, universities, workplaces, and communities.

Recyclable materials such as:

* Plastic
* Paper
* Metal
* Glass

are often mixed with general waste. Many institutions also lack an effective way to track recycling participation and motivate people to recycle consistently.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

EcoBin Rewards helps users:

1. Identify recyclable waste.
2. Verify the waste category using AI-assisted technology.
3. Dispose of eligible waste correctly.
4. Earn EcoPoints for recycling activities.
5. Track their recycling contribution.
6. Redeem points for available rewards.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🤖 AI Waste Verification

AI-assisted technology helps identify and classify recyclable waste categories.

### ⭐ EcoPoints Rewards System

Users earn digital EcoPoints for eligible recycling activities.

### 📊 Recycling Impact Tracking

Users and institutions can track recycling participation and environmental contributions.

### 🏆 Campus Leaderboards

Students and teams can participate in friendly recycling competitions.

### 🎁 Rewards

EcoPoints can be redeemed for available rewards and benefits.

### 🏫 Campus Dashboard

Educational institutions can monitor recycling participation and performance.
